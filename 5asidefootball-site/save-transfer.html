5 Aside Manager · Save transfer

Move your club progress

Saves stay on the device and website where you played. Transfer your season, players, tactics and finances from the old Vercel address to playcellbound.com using the same saved game text.

1. On your old website

Open this page on your old Vercel game address, then select Export save. Copy the displayed text.

2. On your new website

Open this same page on playcellbound.com, paste your copied text into the box, and select Import save. Any existing local save at this new address will be backed up first.

Open the game

This transfer runs entirely in your browser. No account, server, or paid service is required. Do not delete your old save until you have checked that your team loads correctly on the new website.
