The game Places Development

A browser RPG about building a guild

Choose your five.
Then live with the choice.

Cellbound is about the bit before the fight as much as the fight itself — who you bring, what they are wearing, what they can make and whether the party you built can actually handle what is waiting inside.

See what the game is becoming ↓

Pre-alpha · public access stays closed while the game is still moving around underneath us.

Current focus

Getting the combat, UI and artwork to feel like they all belong to the same game.

The short version

You do not play one hero.
You run the whole guild.

You recruit the roster, pick an active party of five and decide how that group is built. Tanks, healers and damage dealers all have a job, but the interesting part is making five characters work together rather than chasing one perfect build.

Between runs you are back in the guild: sorting gear, crafting, changing the party, taking quests and deciding what is worth attempting next.

During a fight

The party keeps moving.
You step in when it matters.

Combat runs in real time. Your characters handle their normal attacks and abilities while you deal with priority targets, interrupts, defensive calls and the moments where the plan starts falling apart.

Target the problem. Boss mechanic, healer, add, whatever needs attention now.

Spend commands carefully. They are there to change a fight, not to become another button-mashing rotation.

Watch the whole party. Health, resources, threat, summons and positioning all matter at the same time.

Back at the guild

Most of the decisions happen before you press Enter.

01

Roster

Build more than one useful team. Different classes, different roles, different answers when a dungeon starts asking awkward questions.

02

Gear

Items roll differently, better tiers change how a character develops, and the best piece on paper is not always the piece your party actually needs.

03

Professions

Every character gets one. Craft gear, consumables and attachments that make preparation part of the run instead of a menu you ignore.

04

The wider game

Quests, trading, activities, world bosses, raids and social systems all feed back into the same roster rather than living as separate mini-games.

Places we have actually built

Not five versions of the same corridor.

Each dungeon is being treated like a place with its own mood and mechanics, not a background swap around the same encounter.

01

Ashen Vault

Hot, cramped and getting worse the deeper you go. The Vaultheart is waiting at the bottom.

02

Hollow Sanctum

Three rooms, a broken sacred place and a choir that should probably have stayed quiet.

03

Chaos Canyon

Green, overgrown and increasingly unstable. One bad crossing can make the next boss harder.

04

Blackout Station

An abandoned power station full of bad wiring, worse ideas and Dr Vex Calder.

05

Fractured Ages

Western town, medieval castle, ancient kingdom, space and a funhouse. It is meant to feel a little unhinged.

The first raid

The Manor

Two players bring five characters each. The raid splits both parties through different parts of the house, then forces them back together with the consequences of what happened while they were apart.

Ten characters sounds like chaos. That is partly the point.

Outside the dungeon list

Zeltira needs to feel like somewhere you return to.

The long-term direction is a proper living hub rather than a stack of menus: places to craft, trade, pick up quests, meet other players and head back out again.

Some of that exists already. Some of it is still sketches, rough systems and half-finished ideas. The site will change as the game does.

A note on development

No fake trailer. No pretend launch date.

Cellbound is still pre-alpha. Combat is being rebuilt, old artwork is being replaced, the UI is being tightened up and content is still changing. The public game is staying closed until those pieces feel like one coherent thing.

This website is here to show the direction without pretending the game is further along than it is.

When there is something worth playing publicly, the button will appear here.

For now

The gates stay shut.

We keep building.
