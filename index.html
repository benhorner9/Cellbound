THE GAME

Your party fights.
You make the calls.

Cellbound puts you in charge of a growing guild rather than a single hero. Build a roster around different classes and roles, decide who earns a place in your active party, then take that group into content built around preparation, timing and adaptation.

Progress does not stop when a fight ends. Equipment, professions, quests, trading and the wider guild all feed back into the next expedition.

REAL-TIME PARTY COMBAT Five adventurers. One command view.

CURRENT STATUS

Cellbound is being built in public view,
but not released yet.

The game is currently in pre-alpha development. Systems, balance, artwork and content are still changing, so public game access stays closed while the playable build is tested separately.

This site is the public home for Cellbound. The game itself will only be connected here once it is ready for players.
