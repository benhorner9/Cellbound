Episode 1: quests across Zeltira, five main dungeons, The Manor raid and the Null Complex finale.

The short version

Five adventurers.
One guild to look after.

You recruit the roster, pick an active party of five and decide how that group is built. Tanks, healers and damage dealers all have a job, but the interesting part is making five characters work together rather than chasing one perfect build.

After a run, head back to your guild. Sort the loot, craft supplies, change your talents and prepare for the next expedition.

During a fight

Your party fights.
You make the calls.

Your characters fight in real time. Call targets, interrupt dangerous abilities and change tactics when a boss forces your hand.

Target the problem. Boss mechanic, healer, add, whatever needs attention now.

Spend commands carefully. They are there to change a fight, not to become another button-mashing rotation.

Watch the whole party. Health, resources, threat, summons and positioning all matter at the same time.

EPISODE ONE · DUNGEONS

Five dungeons. Five different problems.

Start in the Ashen Vault and work towards the Fractured Ages. Each dungeon has its own setting, bosses and mechanics.

01

Ashen Vault

Hot, cramped and getting worse the deeper you go. The Vaultheart is waiting at the bottom.

02

Hollow Sanctum

Three rooms, a broken sacred place and a choir that should probably have stayed quiet.

03

Chaos Canyon

Green, overgrown and increasingly unstable. One bad crossing can make the next boss harder.

04

Blackout Station

An abandoned power station full of bad wiring, worse ideas and Dr Vex Calder.

05

Fractured Ages

Western town, medieval castle, ancient kingdom, space and a funhouse. It is meant to feel a little unhinged.

The first raid

The Manor

Two players bring five characters each. The raid splits both parties through different parts of the house, then forces them back together with the consequences of what happened while they were apart.

Bring your best party, find an ally and work together to clear the house.

Outside the dungeon list

Zeltira needs to feel like somewhere you return to.

Return to Zeltira between adventures to manage your roster, visit the market, prepare equipment and pick up quests.

Episode 1 is the starting point. New stories and areas will come later, after they are ready to play.

How to play

Make a party. Take a quest. Bring them home.

Create your guild and recruit five adventurers. Choose a tank, a healer and damage dealers, then work through the tutorial and the first quest, Ashes on the East Road.

Clear dungeons for equipment, improve your characters with talents and professions, and prepare for harder fights. Finish the Episode 1 storyline to discover what happened inside the Null Complex.

After the story, try Heroic dungeons and Cellbound+ challenges. Compare clear times, work towards rare drops and claim weekly rewards.

Play in your browser. No separate download required. Create your guild →
